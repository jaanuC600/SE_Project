===== Requirements for: Loan Eligibility Check =====
FR-01 [Functional]: The system shall check loan eligibility based on the borrower's income, credit score, and employment history.
Reasoning: Lenders need to assess creditworthiness before approving a loan.

FR-02 [Functional]: The system shall consider RBI guidelines for loan-to-income ratio and credit-to-deposit ratio.
Reasoning: RBI regulations govern lending practices in India.

FR-03 [Functional]: The system shall validate borrower documents, such as ID proof, address proof, and income proof.
Reasoning: Lenders need to verify borrower identity and income to ensure loan eligibility.

FR-04 [Functional]: The system shall generate a credit report from a recognized credit agency, such as CIBIL or Equifax.
Reasoning: Credit reports provide valuable information on borrower credit history.

FR-05 [Functional]: The system shall display loan eligibility details, including the approved amount, interest rate, and repayment terms.
Reasoning: Borrowers need to understand the terms of their loan before accepting it.

===== Requirements for: Credit Scoring =====
FR-01 [Functional]: The system shall calculate the credit score for each borrower based on their credit history, income, and other relevant factors.
Reasoning: A credit score helps lenders assess the creditworthiness of borrowers.

FR-02 [Functional]: The system shall categorize borrowers into different credit score bands (e.g., good, fair, bad) and provide a corresponding risk rating.
Reasoning: Different credit score bands require different risk management strategies.

FR-03 [Functional]: The system shall update the credit score and risk rating in real-time as new credit information becomes available.
Reasoning: Credit scores and risk ratings are dynamic and need to reflect changes in a borrower's credit history.

FR-04 [Functional]: The system shall provide a clear explanation of how the credit score was calculated, including the weights assigned to each factor.
Reasoning: Borrowers need to understand how their credit score was determined.

FR-05 [Functional]: The system shall ensure that credit scores and risk ratings comply with RBI guidelines and Indian data protection law.
Reasoning: Regulatory requirements must be followed to maintain the integrity of the credit scoring process.

NFR-01 [Non-functional]: The system shall calculate credit scores and update risk ratings within 2 seconds.
Reasoning: Fast credit scoring and risk rating updates are essential for real-time lending decisions.
