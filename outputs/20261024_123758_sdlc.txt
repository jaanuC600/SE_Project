Based on the given requirements, I recommend the Spiral SDLC model. Here's why:

1.  **Requirement Stability**: The requirements for Loan Eligibility Check and Credit Scoring are relatively stable, as they are functional and non-functional requirements that are well-defined and well-understood. The requirements do not change frequently, and the system needs to meet the requirements exactly.
2.  **Risk**: The system needs to ensure that only eligible borrowers are approved for loans, and the system needs to calculate the credit score accurately and consistently. The risk is high if the system fails to meet these requirements, as it can result in financial losses and reputational damage.
3.  **Regulatory Needs**: The system needs to comply with regulatory requirements, such as data protection and anti-money laundering regulations. The Spiral SDLC model is well-suited for projects that require regulatory compliance, as it allows for iterative development and testing.
4.  **Need for Feedback**: The system needs to provide feedback to the user, such as a report of loan eligibility and credit score. The Spiral SDLC model allows for iterative development and testing, which ensures that the system meets the requirements and provides the required feedback.

The closest alternative is the Waterfall SDLC model. However, the Waterfall model is less suitable for this project because:

*   The requirements are not well-defined, as they are functional and non-functional requirements that are subject to change.
*   The system needs to meet the requirements exactly, and the Waterfall model does not allow for iterative development and testing.
*   The Waterfall model does not allow for the incorporation of new requirements during the development phase, which can result in the system not meeting the requirements.

In summary, the Spiral SDLC model is the most appropriate SDLC model for this project, as it allows for iterative development and testing, ensures that the system meets the requirements, and provides feedback to the user.
